# Wording rules (read before writing a summary, bullets or a letter)

Standing choices about how this person is described. Follow them exactly; they override style preferences.

**Years.** The figures that may be quoted, each attached to what it measures (for example "12+ years in IT and technical support, about 7 in systems engineering").

**Titles.** Real titles, and the framings a resume may use (also listed as `framings` on each role in facts.md).

**Voice.** No first person in resume bullets, tense, register, punctuation, words to avoid.

**Scope limits.** Anything that must be described as support rather than ownership, proof of concept rather than production, and so on.

**Conditional wording.** Facts that belong only on certain kinds of postings (mark those atoms `only_for` in facts.md).
